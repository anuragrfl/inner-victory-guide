Privacy Policy - Inner Victory Guide

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Inner Victory Guide is a WhatsApp chatbot that shares spiritual guidance on the Devis, chakras, Nidhis, Siddhis and the ten vices.

What we receive

When you message the bot, WhatsApp (Meta) passes us your phone number, your profile name and the text of your message, so the bot can reply.

How we use it

Your message is sent to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) only to write a reply. We do not sell, share or use your information for advertising.

Storage

To remember the flow of your conversation, the bot keeps a short recent chat history linked to your WhatsApp number, stored with our hosting provider (Netlify). It is used only to reply to you. Netlify also keeps technical logs for up to 24 hours.

Deleting your data

To have your chat history deleted, send the message "DELETE MY DATA" to the bot's WhatsApp number. We will remove it within 7 days. You can also stop at any time by no longer messaging or blocking the number.
